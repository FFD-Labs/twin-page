How this page works

This page is one file: its code, its fonts and the addresses it reads from. The bag comes in the link, after the #: a network, a contract and a bag number.

With a bag, your device reads two public networks:

Ethereum
Where the bag’s records are anchored, and who holds its twin.
Swarm
The records, each checked on your device against its anchor, the files they name, each checked against the reference its record gives, and how long Swarm is paid to keep them.

It also loads a holder’s ENS picture from wherever they keep it, and asks for this file once more to compute its fingerprint.

Without a bag, it asks neither of them for anything.

To see how, view the source of this file. A map at its head names its parts in order: 1 · Styles, 2 · Markup, 3 · Config, 4 · Library, 5 · Script and 6 · Fonts.
